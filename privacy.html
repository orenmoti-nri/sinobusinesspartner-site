Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 July 2026 · Last updated: 20 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sino Business Partner ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information in connection with our website sinobusinesspartner.com and our services. It is designed to reflect the requirements of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Israel's Privacy Protection Law (including Amendment No. 13, in force from August 2025), and the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Plain-language summary: We run a simple website. We do not sell your personal information. We only load marketing/analytics cookies (the Meta Pixel) after you accept them. If you contact us, we use your details to respond to you. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@sinobusinesspartner.com.

Sino Business Partner is a business-travel design desk based in Tel Aviv, Israel. For any privacy matter you can reach us at:

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide to us

Our website does not currently include data-entry forms. If you choose to contact us by email, WhatsApp, WeChat or phone, you may share information such as your name, email address, phone number, company, and details of your travel or business needs. We receive only the information you decide to send us.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you visit the website, limited technical information may be processed:

  • Server / security logs (via our hosting provider Cloudflare): IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, referring page, and timestamps — used to operate and secure the site.
  • Marketing & analytics (only after consent): the Meta Pixel sets cookies (e.g. _fbp) and reports website activity to Meta to measure the performance of our social-media presence.
  • Consent preference: your cookie choice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so we can remember it.

4. Why we use your information (purposes & legal bases)

  • To respond to your enquiries and take steps at your request before entering into an agreement — legal basis: legitimate interest / pre-contractual steps (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)/(f)).
  • To measure and improve our marketing via the Meta Pixel — legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • To operate, secure and maintain the website — legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers ("processors") that help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Cloudflare — website hosting, content delivery and security.
  • Meta Platforms — the Meta Pixel, if you consent to marketing cookies.
  • Zoho — business email (if you email us).
  • Trusted service partners in China — only where necessary to plan and deliver a trip you have engaged us to arrange.

6. International data transfers

We are based in Israel and work globally. Some providers (e.g. Meta, Cloudflare) may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, for the US, the providers' certifications. Israel is recognized by the EU as providing an adequate level of data protection.

7. How long we keep your information

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain our business relationship, and thereafter only as required to meet legal, accounting or security obligations. Cookie/pixel data is retained according to the relevant provider's retention periods.

8. How we protect your information

In line with the security duty under Israel's Privacy Protection Law (Amendment 13) and the GDPR, we apply reasonable technical and organizational measures — including HTTPS encryption, access controls and reputable service providers — to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

9.1 If you are in the EEA or UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9.2 If you are in Israel (Privacy Protection Law)

You have the right to review information we hold about you, to request that inaccurate information be corrected or completed, and to request deletion. To exercise these rights, contact us at the details above.

9.3 If you are in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request access and deletion, to correct inaccurate information, to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell personal information. Use of the Meta Pixel may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CPRA — you can opt out simply by clicking Decline on the cookie banner.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@sinobusinesspartner.com. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.
